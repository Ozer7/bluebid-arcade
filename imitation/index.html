Imitation

Two minutes on the wire with a stranger. Then you guess: person, or Claude?

How a round works

  • Press Find a match. Matchmaking takes a few seconds either way, so how long you wait gives nothing away.
  • If another player is searching at the same time, you're paired with them. If not, Claude plays the other side and tries to pass as a person.
  • You take turns, like the game Human or Not: one message each, up to 100 characters, 20 seconds to send it or your turn passes.
  • Chat for 2:00. You can lock in a guess early after 0:45.
  • Guess Human or AI. If you played a person, they're guessing about you too.
